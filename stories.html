Old Testament · Creation

The Creation Story

Discover how God created the heavens, the earth and everything in them.

Genesis 1–2

In the beginning, there was nothing but darkness. Then God began to create. He made light and separated it from darkness.

He made the sky, the land and the seas. He filled the earth with plants, trees and living creatures. He made the sun, moon and stars, as well as birds, fish and animals.

Finally, God created human beings in His image. He saw everything He had made, and it was very good. On the seventh day, God rested.

Camp Lesson: Creation reminds us that the world is precious and that everything has a beginning in God.

Old Testament · Faith

Noah and the Ark

A man, a giant boat, two of every kind of animal and an incredible storm.

Genesis 6–9

God saw that the world had become full of wickedness. But Noah was a righteous man who walked with God.

God instructed Noah to build a great ark. Noah followed God's instructions and brought his family and the animals into the ark.

Then the rain began. It continued for forty days and forty nights. Noah, his family and the animals were safe inside the ark.

When the waters went down, God placed a rainbow in the sky as a sign of His covenant with the earth.

Camp Lesson: Noah teaches us about obedience, faith and God's promises.

Old Testament · Courage

David and Goliath

How a young shepherd faced a mighty warrior with nothing but a sling and faith.

1 Samuel 17

The Israelites were facing the Philistines. Their greatest warrior was Goliath, a giant who challenged anyone to fight him.

Everyone was afraid. But David, a young shepherd, believed that God would help him.

David refused the king's armour. Instead, he took his sling and five smooth stones. He approached Goliath in the name of the Lord.

David launched a stone with his sling. It struck Goliath, who fell to the ground. The Israelites won the battle.

Camp Lesson: Courage isn't about being the biggest or strongest. It is about trusting God when something seems impossible.

Old Testament · Faith

Daniel in the Lions' Den

Daniel refused to abandon his faith, even when doing so put his life at risk.

Daniel 6

Daniel was a respected official in the kingdom. Some jealous officials wanted to find a reason to punish him.

They convinced the king to make a law forbidding anyone from praying to anyone other than the king for thirty days.

Daniel continued praying to God three times a day. He was arrested and thrown into a den of lions.

But God sent an angel to shut the lions' mouths. Daniel was found unharmed the next morning.

Camp Lesson: Daniel teaches us to remain faithful even when it is difficult.

Old Testament · Obedience

Jonah and the Great Fish

A reluctant prophet, a terrible storm and an extraordinary encounter at sea.

Jonah 1–4

God told Jonah to go to Nineveh and warn its people about their wicked behaviour. But Jonah did not want to go.

Instead, he boarded a ship travelling in the opposite direction. A terrible storm struck the sea.

Jonah eventually found himself thrown into the sea, where a great fish swallowed him. He remained inside the fish for three days and three nights.

Jonah prayed to God, and the fish released him onto dry land. He then went to Nineveh and delivered God's message.

Camp Lesson: Jonah's story teaches us about obedience, second chances and God's mercy.

New Testament · Faith

Jesus Walks on Water

A storm, frightened disciples and an extraordinary moment of faith.

Matthew 14:22–33

Jesus sent His disciples ahead of Him in a boat while He went up a mountain to pray.

During the night, a strong wind troubled the boat. Then the disciples saw Jesus walking towards them on the water.

Peter asked Jesus to let him come to Him. Peter stepped out of the boat and began walking towards Jesus.

But when Peter noticed the strong wind, he became afraid and started sinking. Jesus immediately reached out and caught him.

Camp Lesson: Trust Jesus even when life feels frightening.

New Testament · Kindness

The Good Samaritan

A story about kindness, compassion and helping someone who needs you.

Luke 10:25–37

A man was travelling from Jerusalem to Jericho when robbers attacked him. They took his belongings, beat him and left him injured on the road.

A priest passed by without helping. A Levite also saw him but continued walking.

Then a Samaritan came along. He stopped, treated the man's wounds and took him to an inn. He even paid for his care.

Jesus told this story to teach that our neighbour is anyone who needs our kindness.

Camp Lesson: Love is shown through our actions, not just our words.

New Testament · Love

The Lost Sheep

A shepherd leaves ninety-nine sheep to find the one that has wandered away.

Luke 15:1–7

Jesus told a story about a shepherd who had one hundred sheep.

When one went missing, he left the ninety-nine and went searching for the lost one.

When he found it, he joyfully carried it home on his shoulders.

He called his friends and neighbours together to celebrate because the lost sheep had been found.

Camp Lesson: God's love reaches every person. No one is forgotten.

New Testament · Hope

The Resurrection of Jesus

The story of the empty tomb and the discovery that Jesus had risen.

Matthew 28; Mark 16; Luke 24; John 20

After Jesus was crucified and buried, His followers were heartbroken.

On the third day, some women went to the tomb. They discovered that the stone had been moved and that the tomb was empty.

An angel told them that Jesus had risen, just as He had promised.

Jesus later appeared to His followers, bringing them hope and joy.

Camp Lesson: For Christians, the resurrection is the foundation of their hope and faith.
