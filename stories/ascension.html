FAITH AND PURPOSE

The Ascension of Jesus

Luke 24:36–53; Acts 1:1–12

After rising from the dead, Jesus spent time with His disciples, teaching them and preparing them for the work they would continue. Then, before their eyes, He returned to heaven.

Jesus Spends Time with His Disciples

After His resurrection, Jesus appeared to His followers on several occasions. He showed them that He was truly alive and helped them understand the meaning of everything that had happened.

He spoke to them about God's kingdom and explained how the Scriptures had pointed towards His suffering and resurrection.

Jesus wanted His disciples to understand that their work was not finished. They would soon have an important responsibility: sharing His message with people throughout the world.

The Promise of the Holy Spirit

Before leaving them, Jesus instructed His disciples not to leave Jerusalem immediately. He told them to wait for the gift God had promised.

Jesus explained that they would receive power when the Holy Spirit came upon them. They would become His witnesses in Jerusalem, throughout Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth.

This promise gave the disciples hope. They would not have to carry out their mission alone.

Jesus Ascends into Heaven

Jesus led His disciples out towards the area of Bethany, near the Mount of Olives.

He lifted His hands and blessed them. As He was blessing them, He was taken up into heaven.

In the account in Acts, a cloud received Him from their sight. The disciples stood looking up towards the sky.

Suddenly, two men dressed in white appeared beside them. They asked why they were standing there looking into heaven.

They explained that Jesus, who had been taken into heaven, would return in the same way they had seen Him go.

The Disciples Return to Jerusalem

The disciples returned to Jerusalem with joy. They gathered together, praying and waiting for the promise Jesus had made to them.

They knew that although Jesus was no longer physically with them, their mission was only beginning.

They were about to receive the Holy Spirit and begin spreading the message of Jesus far beyond Jerusalem.

Why the Ascension Matters

Christians believe that Jesus' ascension marked His return to the Father in heaven. It also marked a new chapter for His followers.

Jesus had given them a mission: to teach, serve, make disciples, and share the good news with the world.

The disciples would soon begin carrying out that mission, supported by the Holy Spirit.

What Can We Learn?

  • Jesus gave His followers a purpose and a responsibility.
  • We can trust God's promises, even when we do not know exactly what will happen next.
  • Faith should inspire us to take action and help others.
  • We do not have to face every challenge alone.
  • The disciples' mission to share the message of Jesus continues to inspire Christians today.

Think About It!

If you had been one of the disciples watching Jesus ascend into heaven, what might you have been thinking? How can you share kindness, hope, and faith with the people around you?
