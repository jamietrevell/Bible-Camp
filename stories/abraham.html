FAITH AND PROMISE

Abraham: A Journey of Faith

Genesis 12–22

God called a man to leave everything familiar and journey towards an unknown land. His name was Abram, later called Abraham, and his story would become one of the Bible's great stories of faith.

A Call to Leave Home

Abram lived in the land of Haran with his wife Sarai and his household. One day, God spoke to him and gave him an extraordinary instruction: he was to leave his country, his relatives, and his father's household and travel to a land God would show him.

God made him a remarkable promise. He would make Abram into a great nation, bless him, make his name great, and bless all the families of the earth through him.

Abram obeyed. He gathered Sarai, his nephew Lot, their possessions, and the people who travelled with them. Together, they set out for Canaan.

A Promise Beneath the Stars

Although Abram had received God's promise, he and Sarai had no children. As the years passed, Abram wondered how he could become the father of a great nation without an heir.

God reassured him. One night, God brought Abram outside and told him to look up at the stars. He promised that Abram's descendants would be as numerous as the stars in the sky.

Abram believed God, and his faith was counted to him as righteousness.

A New Name and a Covenant

God renewed His promise and established a covenant with Abram. He promised that Abram would become the father of many nations.

God changed his name from Abram to Abraham. Sarai's name was also changed to Sarah.

God promised that Sarah would have a son, even though both she and Abraham were very old. Through this son, Isaac, the covenant would continue.

The Birth of Isaac

At first, the promise seemed impossible. Abraham was about one hundred years old, and Sarah was ninety. Yet God did exactly what He had promised.

Sarah gave birth to a son, and they named him Isaac. Their joy was immense. The child they had waited so long for had finally arrived.

Isaac's birth reminded Abraham and Sarah that God's promises were not limited by what seemed possible to human beings.

A Difficult Test

Years later, God tested Abraham's faith. He told Abraham to take Isaac to the land of Moriah and offer him there as a burnt offering.

Abraham set out early the next morning with Isaac and two servants. They travelled until they reached the place God had shown him.

As they climbed the mountain, Isaac noticed that they had wood and fire but no lamb for the offering. He asked his father where the lamb was.

Abraham answered that God would provide the lamb.

Abraham built an altar and prepared to offer Isaac. But before he could harm his son, an angel of the Lord called out and stopped him.

Abraham saw a ram caught by its horns in a nearby thicket. He offered the ram instead.

God had provided, and Isaac was spared. Abraham named the place to remember God's provision.

A Legacy of Faith

Abraham's journey was not always easy. He faced uncertainty, difficult decisions, and long periods of waiting. But his story became an important example of trusting God.

God continued His covenant with Abraham, promising that his descendants would become a great nation and that all nations of the earth would be blessed through his offspring.

What Can We Learn?

  • Faith sometimes means trusting God without knowing every detail.
  • Waiting can be difficult, but patience matters.
  • God's promises are central to Abraham's story.
  • Abraham's story reminds us that faith can be tested.
  • We should remember to be grateful when help and provision come.

Think About It!

Abraham was asked to leave the familiar and travel somewhere unknown. Is there something new you would find difficult to try because you do not know what will happen?
