FORGIVENESS AND LOVE

The Prodigal Son

Luke 15:11–32

A young man demanded his inheritance, left home, and wasted everything he had. But when he finally returned, expecting rejection, he discovered something he had never expected: his father was waiting for him with open arms.

A Father and His Two Sons

Jesus told a story about a man who had two sons. The younger son was restless and wanted to experience life away from home.

One day, he approached his father and demanded his share of the family inheritance. The father agreed and divided his property between his two sons.

The younger son gathered everything he owned and left for a distant country. He wanted to live independently, far away from his family.

Wasting Everything

In the distant country, the younger son began spending his money carelessly. He lived extravagantly and wasted his inheritance on things that brought him temporary pleasure.

Before long, he had spent everything. Then a severe famine struck the country, and he found himself in desperate need.

He approached a local citizen, who sent him into the fields to feed pigs.

The young man became so hungry that even the food given to the pigs looked appealing. Nobody was giving him anything.

A Moment of Realisation

Eventually, the young man came to his senses. He remembered that his father's hired workers had more than enough food, while he was starving in a foreign land.

He decided to return home.

He planned what he would say: he would admit that he had sinned against heaven and against his father. He would no longer ask to be treated as a son. Instead, he would ask to become one of his father's hired workers.

With nothing left, he began the long journey home.

A Father Who Was Waiting

While the younger son was still a long way off, his father saw him approaching.

The father was filled with compassion. He ran towards his son, embraced him, and kissed him.

The son began his prepared confession. He admitted that he had sinned and was no longer worthy to be called his father's son.

But the father did not reject him. Instead, he called to his servants and ordered them to bring the finest robe, a ring, and sandals for his son.

He also ordered that a fattened calf be prepared for a celebration.

The father announced that they should celebrate because his son had been dead and was alive again; he had been lost and was now found.

The Older Brother's Anger

Meanwhile, the older son had been working in the fields. As he approached the house, he heard music and dancing.

He asked a servant what was happening and learned that his younger brother had returned safely.

The older brother became angry. He refused to enter the house.

His father came outside and pleaded with him to join the celebration.

But the older son complained that he had worked faithfully for years and had never received such a celebration. Yet his younger brother, who had wasted his inheritance, was being welcomed home with a feast.

The Father's Explanation

The father gently reminded his older son that he had always been with him and that everything belonging to the father was also available to him.

But celebrating the younger son's return was necessary. His brother had been lost and was now found. He had been as good as dead, but he had returned home alive.

Jesus ended the story without telling us whether the older brother joined the celebration. The ending invites us to consider how we respond when someone who has made mistakes is forgiven and welcomed back.

What the Story Means

Jesus told this parable to teach about God's love and forgiveness. The father represents a loving God who welcomes those who turn back to Him.

The younger son shows us that people can make serious mistakes, recognise them, and choose a different path.

The older brother reminds us that resentment and jealousy can prevent us from celebrating another person's happiness.

Above all, the story teaches that genuine repentance can be met with mercy and joy.

What Can We Learn?

  • Our choices have consequences, and we should take responsibility for them.
  • It is never too late to admit our mistakes and choose a better path.
  • Forgiveness can restore relationships.
  • We should not allow jealousy to stop us from celebrating someone else's growth.
  • Love does not mean pretending mistakes never happened; it means showing compassion and welcoming change.
  • Everyone deserves the opportunity to seek forgiveness and begin again.

Think About It!

Imagine being the older brother. Would you have found it easy to welcome your younger brother home? What does this story teach us about forgiveness, jealousy, and giving people another chance?
