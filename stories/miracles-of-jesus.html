MIRACLES AND COMPASSION

The Miracles of Jesus

Matthew, Mark, Luke and John

Wherever Jesus travelled, people came to hear Him. They brought their sick, their worries, and their questions. Again and again, Jesus showed His power through extraordinary miracles and His compassion for those who needed help.

Water Becomes Wine

Jesus and His disciples attended a wedding in Cana of Galilee. His mother, Mary, was also there. During the celebration, the hosts ran out of wine.

Mary told Jesus about the problem. She then instructed the servants to do whatever He told them.

Nearby stood six large stone water jars. Jesus instructed the servants to fill them with water. They filled them to the brim.

He then told them to draw some out and take it to the person in charge of the feast. When the man tasted it, he discovered that the water had become wine.

It was the first of Jesus' signs recorded in the Gospel of John. His disciples saw His glory and believed in Him.

Healing the Sick

Jesus travelled through towns and villages, teaching about God's kingdom. Wherever He went, people brought those who were sick or suffering.

A man with leprosy once approached Jesus. He knelt before Him and said that Jesus could make him clean if He was willing.

Jesus reached out and touched him, saying that He was willing. Immediately, the man was healed.

On another occasion, a paralyzed man was brought to Jesus by four friends. The crowd around the house was so large that they could not reach the entrance.

Instead, they opened the roof above Jesus and lowered their friend down on a mat. Jesus saw their faith and first told the man that his sins were forgiven.

Some religious teachers questioned Jesus' authority to forgive sins. To show them that He had authority, Jesus told the man to get up, pick up his mat, and go home. The man stood up, picked up his mat, and walked out in front of everyone.

Jesus Calms the Storm

One evening, Jesus and His disciples climbed into a boat to cross the Sea of Galilee. Jesus was tired and fell asleep.

Suddenly, a violent storm arose. Waves crashed over the boat, and the disciples feared they would drown.

They woke Jesus, crying out for help. Jesus stood up and rebuked the wind and the waves.

Immediately, the wind stopped and the water became calm.

Jesus asked His disciples why they were so afraid and where their faith was. They were amazed and asked one another who He was, since even the wind and sea obeyed Him.

Feeding Five Thousand People

Large crowds followed Jesus because they wanted to hear His teaching and see His miracles. One day, a huge crowd gathered in a remote place. As evening approached, the people became hungry.

The disciples suggested sending everyone away to find food. But Jesus told them to give the people something to eat.

A boy had brought five small loaves of bread and two fish. Andrew brought the food to Jesus, although he wondered how it could possibly feed such a large crowd.

Jesus instructed the people to sit down. He took the loaves and fish, gave thanks, and distributed them to the people.

Everyone ate until they were satisfied. When the disciples collected the leftovers, they filled twelve baskets with the remaining food.

About five thousand men had been fed, not including the women and children.

Jesus Walks on Water

After feeding the crowd, Jesus sent His disciples ahead in a boat while He went up a mountain to pray.

During the night, the disciples were struggling against strong winds and waves. Then they saw someone walking towards them on the water.

They were terrified, thinking they were seeing a ghost. But Jesus spoke to them and told them not to be afraid.

Peter called out, asking Jesus to command him to come onto the water. Jesus told him to come, and Peter stepped out of the boat.

For a moment, Peter walked towards Jesus. But when he noticed the strong wind, he became frightened and began to sink.

He cried out for help, and Jesus immediately reached out His hand and caught him. Together they climbed into the boat, and the wind died down.

Jesus Raises Lazarus

In the village of Bethany lived two sisters, Martha and Mary, and their brother Lazarus. They were close friends of Jesus.

One day, Lazarus became seriously ill. His sisters sent word to Jesus, but by the time He arrived, Lazarus had already been in the tomb for four days.

Martha went out to meet Jesus. She told Him that if He had been there, her brother would not have died.

Jesus told her that Lazarus would rise again and spoke about being the resurrection and the life.

When Mary came to Jesus, she was weeping. Seeing her grief and the sadness of those around her, Jesus was deeply moved. He wept too.

Jesus went to the tomb and asked for the stone covering its entrance to be removed. Martha warned Him that there would be an unpleasant smell because Lazarus had been dead for four days.

After praying, Jesus called out in a loud voice, "Lazarus, come forth!"

Lazarus came out of the tomb, still wrapped in burial cloths. Jesus told the people to unwrap him and let him go.

Many people who witnessed this miracle believed in Jesus.

Why Did Jesus Perform Miracles?

Jesus' miracles were not simply displays of power. They showed His compassion for people who were suffering, hungry, frightened, or grieving.

They also revealed His authority and helped people understand who He was. Through His actions, Jesus demonstrated God's love and the arrival of His kingdom.

What Can We Learn?

  • Jesus showed compassion towards people who were suffering.
  • We can turn to God when we are frightened or uncertain.
  • Faith can give us courage during difficult situations.
  • Sharing what we have can make a difference to others.
  • Jesus cared about people's physical needs as well as their spiritual lives.
  • We should show kindness to people who are hurting or grieving.

Think About It!

The boy shared his small meal, and Jesus used it to feed thousands of people. What do you have that might seem small but could still make a big difference in someone else's life?
