HOPE AND PROMISE

The Birth of Jesus

Matthew 1–2; Luke 1–2

In a small town called Bethlehem, a child was born whose arrival had been promised for generations. His name was Jesus, and His birth would change the world.

An Angel Visits Mary

In the town of Nazareth lived a young woman named Mary. She was engaged to a man named Joseph, who was a descendant of King David.

One day, God sent the angel Gabriel to visit Mary. The angel greeted her and told her not to be afraid. God had chosen her for something extraordinary: she would give birth to a son and name Him Jesus.

Gabriel explained that the child would be great and would be called the Son of the Most High. His kingdom would never end.

Mary was surprised and asked how this could happen, since she was not yet married. Gabriel explained that the Holy Spirit would come upon her and that the child would be holy.

Mary accepted the message, saying that she was the Lord's servant and that His word should be fulfilled.

Joseph's Dream

When Joseph learned that Mary was expecting a child, he was troubled. He was a righteous man and did not want to disgrace her publicly. He considered ending their engagement quietly.

But while Joseph was thinking about this, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream. The angel told him not to be afraid to take Mary as his wife.

The child had been conceived through the Holy Spirit, and Joseph was to name Him Jesus, because He would save His people from their sins.

Joseph woke up and did as the angel had instructed. He took Mary as his wife and cared for her.

The Journey to Bethlehem

Around this time, Caesar Augustus ordered a census throughout the Roman Empire. People had to travel to their ancestral towns to register.

Joseph belonged to the family of David, whose ancestral home was Bethlehem. He travelled there from Nazareth with Mary, who was expecting a baby.

When they arrived, the town was crowded. There was no suitable guest room available for them.

Jesus Is Born

While they were in Bethlehem, the time came for Mary to give birth.

She gave birth to her firstborn son, wrapped Him in cloths, and laid Him in a manger because there was no guest room available for them.

The Son of God had entered the world not in a palace or surrounded by royal riches, but in humble circumstances.

Mary treasured these events and thought deeply about them in her heart.

The Shepherds Hear the News

Nearby, shepherds were staying in the fields, watching over their flocks at night.

Suddenly, an angel of the Lord appeared, and the glory of the Lord shone around them. The shepherds were terrified.

But the angel told them not to be afraid. He brought good news of great joy for all people: a Saviour had been born in the city of David. He was Christ the Lord.

The angel gave them a sign. They would find a baby wrapped in cloths and lying in a manger.

Suddenly, a great company of heavenly beings appeared, praising God and saying, "Glory to God in the highest heaven, and on earth peace to those on whom His favour rests."

When the angels departed, the shepherds hurried to Bethlehem. They found Mary, Joseph, and the baby lying in the manger, just as the angel had said.

They told others what they had seen and heard. Everyone who heard their story was amazed. The shepherds returned to their fields, glorifying and praising God.

The Visit of the Wise Men

Some time later, wise men from the East arrived in Jerusalem. They had seen a special star and believed it announced the birth of a new king of the Jews.

They asked King Herod where the child was. Herod was troubled by the news and asked the religious teachers where the promised ruler was supposed to be born.

They told him that the Scriptures pointed to Bethlehem.

Herod secretly spoke with the wise men and asked them to return and tell him where the child was, claiming that he wanted to worship Him too.

The wise men continued their journey. The star led them to the place where Jesus was. When they saw Him with Mary, they bowed down and worshipped Him.

They presented Him with gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh.

Later, God warned the wise men in a dream not to return to Herod. They went home by another route.

Joseph Protects His Family

After the wise men left, an angel appeared to Joseph in a dream and warned him that Herod intended to search for the child and kill Him.

Joseph took Mary and Jesus and escaped to Egypt during the night. They remained there until Herod died.

Later, an angel told Joseph that it was safe to return. The family eventually settled in Nazareth, where Jesus grew up.

The birth of Jesus fulfilled promises made long before His arrival. Christians celebrate this event as the beginning of the earthly life of the Saviour.

What Can We Learn?

  • God can work through ordinary people in extraordinary ways.
  • Mary showed faith and willingness to trust God's plan.
  • Joseph showed kindness, courage, and responsibility.
  • Jesus' birth brought a message of hope and joy.
  • The shepherds remind us that good news is for everyone.
  • The wise men remind us to seek what is good and honour what is precious.

Think About It!

Mary and Joseph faced uncertainty, travel, and difficult circumstances, yet they trusted God and cared for Jesus. What does their story teach us about faith when life does not go according to plan?
