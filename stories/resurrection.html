HOPE AND NEW LIFE

The Resurrection of Jesus

Matthew 28; Mark 16; Luke 24; John 20–21

Jesus had been crucified and buried in a tomb. His followers were grieving, believing that their time with Him had come to an end. But on the third day, something extraordinary happened.

The Morning After the Sabbath

Early on the first day of the week, while it was still becoming light, Mary Magdalene and other women went to the tomb where Jesus had been buried.

They had brought spices to prepare His body. They expected to find the entrance closed by the large stone that had been placed there.

But when they arrived, they discovered that the stone had been rolled away. The tomb was open.

The Empty Tomb

When the women entered, they discovered that Jesus' body was missing. They were confused and frightened.

In the Gospel accounts, angels appeared and told them not to be afraid. They explained that Jesus was not there because He had risen, just as He had promised.

The angels reminded the women that Jesus had told His followers He would be handed over, crucified, and rise again on the third day.

The women hurried away from the tomb to tell the disciples what they had seen.

Mary Meets Jesus

Mary Magdalene remained near the tomb, crying because she did not know where Jesus' body had been taken.

She turned and saw someone standing nearby. At first, she thought He was the gardener.

Then He spoke her name: "Mary."

She immediately recognised Him. It was Jesus!

Mary was overwhelmed with joy. She went to tell the disciples that she had seen the risen Lord.

Jesus Appears to His Disciples

Later, Jesus appeared to His disciples. They were gathered together behind locked doors because they were afraid of the authorities.

Jesus stood among them and greeted them with the words, "Peace be with you." He showed them His hands and His side, helping them understand that it really was Him.

The disciples were filled with joy when they realised their teacher and friend was alive.

Thomas Has Questions

One of the disciples, Thomas, was not with the others when Jesus first appeared. When they told him what had happened, he struggled to believe them.

Thomas said he needed to see the marks of the nails and the wound in Jesus' side before he could believe.

A week later, Jesus appeared to the disciples again, and Thomas was there. Jesus invited him to see and touch His wounds.

Thomas responded with faith, declaring, "My Lord and my God!"

Jesus then spoke about those who would believe without seeing Him physically.

Jesus and Peter

After His resurrection, Jesus also met some of His disciples beside the Sea of Galilee.

They had been fishing all night without catching anything. Jesus told them to cast their net on the other side of the boat, and suddenly they caught a huge number of fish.

After breakfast, Jesus spoke privately with Peter.

Peter had denied knowing Jesus three times before the crucifixion. Now Jesus asked him three times whether he loved Him.

Each time Peter answered that he did. Jesus told him to care for His followers and continue His work.

This was an important moment of restoration for Peter. His earlier failure did not mean that he could never serve Jesus again.

A Message of Hope

Jesus' resurrection changed everything for His followers. Their sadness turned into joy, and their fear began to give way to courage.

Before returning to heaven, Jesus instructed His disciples to go and make disciples of all nations, teaching people to follow His commands.

Christians celebrate the resurrection at Easter because they believe Jesus conquered death and offers eternal life to those who believe in Him.

What Can We Learn?

  • Christianity teaches that death is not the end because of Jesus' resurrection.
  • Even when we feel afraid or uncertain, hope can give us courage.
  • It is okay to have questions, just as Thomas did.
  • People who make mistakes can be forgiven and given another opportunity, as Peter was.
  • Jesus' followers were called to share His message of love and hope with the world.

Think About It!

Imagine being one of the disciples when you first heard that Jesus was alive. How do you think you would have felt? What does the resurrection mean to Christians?
